📖 How to Play

1. Set My Player Profile (name, country, jersey colour, jersey number).

2. Practice — solo drills vs the keeper. Score 10 goals to finish a level.

3. Online 1v1 — create a room, share the 4-letter code with a friend. They join from their own device and you both race to 10 goals on identical questions.

4. Correct answer = top-corner goal. Wrong answer = keeper catches the ball.

5. You have 20 seconds per question.
